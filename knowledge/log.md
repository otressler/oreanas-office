Added [backward compatibility](/decisions/backward-compatibility.md) as a proposal with the rules for that point, and a `schemaVersion` on the [battle map](/domain/battle-map.md) layout as cheap preparation.

Added an "As built" section to [hosting](/decisions/hosting.md): Germany West Central, a 1-vCore cap on the free database, a placeholder first image, managed identity for SQL and Blob, CI deploys over OIDC behind `DEPLOY_ENABLED`, and TCP-only health probes. Linked it from the [roadmap](/roadmap.md) and moved the table view there.
