# Update Log

## 2026-10-12
- **Stage 2 passed**: The project owner passed the stage 2 gate; accounts and campaigns are on `main` ([implementation plan](/implementation-plan.md)).
- **Session DM only**: The project owner confirmed that actions at the table belong to the session DM alone: starting and ending encounters, moving creatures and other players' characters, and seeing hidden tokens. Being a `dm` member of the campaign, or its default DM, grants none of them. The default DM keeps the right to start, end and hand over a session they aren't running, so a session can't get stuck when its DM is absent. Updated [session](/domain/session.md), [campaign member](/domain/campaign-member.md) and stages 3 and 4 of the [implementation plan](/implementation-plan.md).

## 2026-10-11
- **Stage 2 built**: Accounts and campaigns are on `stage/2-accounts-campaigns`, waiting for the project owner's gate check ([implementation plan](/implementation-plan.md)). Built in three parallel lanes (identity backend, campaign backend, frontend) on a shared contract, then integrated: invite-only registration, local and Google sign-in, linking, admin invites and reset links, campaigns with join codes, members, characters and sessions. The active session is the session with `status: active`, guarded by a unique index, rather than a column on the campaign ([campaign](/domain/campaign.md)). `npm run test:e2e:backend` runs a two-browser test against the real backend in an `E2E` environment, which alone has an endpoint that creates invites. Production gets the Google client and the admin address through `infra/deploy.ps1`, which now requires `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and `ADMIN_EMAIL`.
- **Stage 2 auth details**: The project owner decided that registration is by invite only: the site admin creates a link valid for a fixed 4 hours that any number of people can use, for local accounts and first Google sign-ins alike, except the admin's own first Google sign-in; open registration was rejected. `isAdmin` is site-wide and granted to the owner's Google address, kept in configuration rather than the repository, only on a Google sign-in. The owner also confirmed: passwords of at least 8 characters with standard lockout, a 180-day sliding cookie, a 6-character join code that `dm` members can replace, players editing their own characters and DMs any, any `dm` member creating sessions with the session DM or default DM activating and ending them, and no leaving or removing members yet. Added [registration invite](/domain/registration-invite.md) and updated [auth](/decisions/auth.md), [user](/domain/user.md), [campaign](/domain/campaign.md), [campaign member](/domain/campaign-member.md), [character](/domain/character.md), [session](/domain/session.md), the [data model](/domain/data-model.md) and stage 2 of the [implementation plan](/implementation-plan.md).
