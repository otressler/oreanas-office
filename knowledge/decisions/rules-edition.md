# Question

Does the group play D&D 5e (2014), 5e (2024), or something else, and does it use the alternating-diagonal variant?

# Affects

The default diagonal setting in [movement distance](/rules/movement-distance.md). Because the setting can be changed per campaign, this doesn't block development.

# Answer

The group plays D&D 5e (2024).
- Equal Cost: Every square moved—whether straight (horizontally/vertically) or diagonally—costs 5 feet of movement.
- Simplicity: This method is fast and keeps combat smooth, though it treats a diagonal step as the same distance as a straight step.
- Corners: Diagonal movement cannot cut straight through the corner of a wall, a large tree, or an obstacle that completely blocks or fills that corner junction

# Consequences

[Movement distance](/rules/movement-distance.md) now counts every step as one square and forbids diagonals past a blocked corner. The app shows distances in meters, not feet: the project owner set one square to 1.5 m. The alternating-diagonal setting was dropped. Whether one blocked side cell is enough to block a diagonal is still open there.
