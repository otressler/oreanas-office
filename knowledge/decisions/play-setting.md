# Question

Is everyone always at the same table, or do some players join remotely?

# Affects

- **One table:** a read-only "table view" for a TV or tablet becomes useful ([roadmap](/roadmap.md)). Voice and video aren't needed.
- **Remote:** pings and maybe a link to an external voice call become more important ([playable map](/features/playable-map.md)).
