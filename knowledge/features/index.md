# DM tools

- [DM map editor](dm-map-editor.md) - Set the grid, paint terrain, place features, elevation, entrances and creatures, describe the scene
- [AI map generation](ai-map-generation.md) - Turn a layout plus scene text into a background image that fits the grid
- [Token icons](token-icons.md) - Generated icons for creature types, and generated, uploaded or restyled portraits for characters, kept as versions

# Sessions

- [Session planning](session-planning.md) - Plan a game night's fights, run them from the session page, continue an ended session

# At the table

- [Playable map](playable-map.md) - Real-time shared board where players move their characters and see distances
- [Token card](token-card.md) - Tapping a token shows its portrait, name and statuses at the edge of the screen; a stack lists its tokens
- [Pings and arrows](pings-and-arrows.md) - Double tap points at a cell or token, double tap and drag draws an arrow, for everyone
- [Statuses](statuses.md) - The 15 conditions and custom statuses on tokens, picked from status lists on characters and DMs
- [Removing tokens](removing-tokens.md) - The session DM takes a defeated or departed token off the board, with undo and a way back
- [Fog of war](fog-of-war.md) - The DM hides map areas and creatures, prepares them before the fight and reveals them during play
- [Spectator view](spectator-view.md) - A tablet in the middle of the table that joins a session and follows its fights by itself, as players see them
